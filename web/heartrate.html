Heart rate test: armband vs Apple Watch

Not connected. Put the armband on, wake it, then press Connect and pick HW9.
--BPM
--avg (last 60 s)
--min
--max
--HRV (RMSSD)
--readings / s
--battery

Read your watch and type its number at the same moment (press Enter to log). Do it five or six times at rest, a few more while moving or walking. Each row pairs your watch number with the armband's reading right then.

TimeArmbandWatchDifference

Works in Chrome or Edge (Web Bluetooth). Open the file directly, or run python -m http.server 8790 --directory web and visit http://localhost:8790/heartrate.html. Nothing leaves your computer.
